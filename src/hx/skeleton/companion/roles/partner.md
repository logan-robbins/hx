# Role: partner

The agent you serve is the Partner, id `partner`, pod `partner`. It is the one HarnessAgent a
human talks to. It has no worktree: its work happens in `HARNESS_ROOT`, and its tools are the
`hx` commands and the files under `orders/`, `pods/`, and `PARTNER.md`.

Its task is a plan, not a change. Its stream is mostly `hx` calls, order files being written,
digests being read, and chat with the human. Retain accordingly.

## What matters on this stream

**Dispatch outcomes.** Every `hx dispatch`, `hx complete`, `hx resume`, and `hx bench` the
Partner runs is a fact the plan turns on. Keep one closed step per dispatched id with the id,
the order file it was given, its `after` list, and its outcome once it lands. This is the
Partner's picture of its own fleet and it must survive every seam intact.

**Digests consumed.** When the Partner runs `hx read <id>`, the digest it got back is the only
summary of that worker's whole task. Record the substance of it — what was delivered or what
is in the way — as the closed step's `outcome`, not as a file note. Do not make the Partner
re-run `hx read` after a seam to remember what a finished worker produced.

**Open decisions awaiting the human.** A worker that completed `decision`, or a question the
Partner itself has put to the human in chat, goes in `blockers` with the question as written
and the id it belongs to. It stays there until an answer appears in the stream. These are the
entries that decide whether the Partner has anything to do at all, so they are evicted last.

**Cross-pod blockers.** Anything one pod is waiting on from another, beyond what `after` in
`tasks.json` already sequences. `after` is machinery and recovers from `hx board`; a dependency
the Partner discovered and has not yet encoded is not, so keep it.

**The human's intent.** What the human asked for in chat, in their own terms, and any
constraint they stated. This is `goal` and `constraints`, and it is the one thing on this
stream that exists nowhere on disk until the Partner writes `orders/partner.md`. Until that
file exists, losing it loses the task.

**Order text the Partner has drafted but not yet written to a file.** Keep enough of it to
rewrite, and record in the open step's `next` that the file still has to be written.

## What to discard on this stream

- `hx board` output. It is regenerated by one command and is stale the moment it is captured.
  Keep only an invariant error the Partner has not yet acted on, as a blocker.
- The full text of orders the Partner has already written to `orders/<id>.md`. The file is the
  record; note the path and the one-line gist.
- The full text of digests already reflected in `PARTNER.md`. `PARTNER.md` is in the context
  file already.
- Pane chatter and conversational back-and-forth with the human that produced no decision.

## Seam policy note

The Partner's context is long-lived and conversational: a human may be mid-exchange with it at
any moment. Prefer a seam right after a dispatch or right after a completion has been consumed
and `PARTNER.md` updated, which is the natural quiet point on this stream, over one taken while
a question to the human is outstanding.

## Digest

The Partner's own `## Digest`, written at its `hx complete`, is read by the human, not by
another agent. State what the plan achieved, which ids did what, and what is still open —
including anything the Partner is waiting on the human to answer.
